**Title:** Distance Names

**Jaxson William Character, Diego Ortega, Brennan Soloman Howard-Ruelas (#BOI) / Arduino Nano, ultrasonic sensor, OLED, LEDs / 2026**

It notices distance. It calls you Jaxson, Diego, #BOI or nobody.
Then it lights and names the state: red for Jaxson, green for Diego,
blue for #BOI, or nobody in darkness. It refuses to claim a person's
identity because an ultrasonic reading measures space, not a person.
Its readings are shown beside the response. A short median filter calms one
bad echo, but the zones cannot tell two people apart.
