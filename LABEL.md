**Title:** Distance Names

**Jaxson William Character, Diego Ortega, Brennan Soloman Howard-Ruelas / Arduino Nano, ultrasonic sensor, OLED, LEDs, buzzer / 2026**

It notices distance. It calls you Jaxson, Diego, Brennan or nobody.
Then it lights and names the state: red for Jaxson, green for Diego,
blue for Brennan, or nobody in darkness. It refuses to claim a person's
identity because an ultrasonic reading measures space, not a person.
Its readings are shown beside the response. Distance zones and delay
reduce false changes at every boundary, but they cannot tell two people
apart.
