The Sensor Layer

Creators: Jaxson Character, Diego Ortega, and Brennan

Materials: Arduino Nano, ultrasonic distance sensor, OLED display, LEDs, piezo speaker, and breadboards.

In 2026, our device notices people and objects based on their distance from the sensor. 
It calls you NOBODY, CLOSE, or FAR. Then it responds using lights, sounds, and words displayed on its screen. 
It refuses to guess beyond what its sensor can detect. Its purpose is to turn distance into an interactive experience.
